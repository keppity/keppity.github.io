Tracking

How do I add something?

Press + Add. Type a title and press Search online to fill in the cover, year, episode count and genres automatically. You can also paste a link from AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, TVmaze, IMDb, RAWG, Open Library or Apple Podcasts into the title box.

For a cover, drop or paste an image onto the cover box. On a phone, copy an image from anywhere and tap Paste copied image.

Where does Search online get its information?

Each type has a main source and a backup: anime and manga from AniList, then Kitsu; TV from TVmaze, then OMDb; movies from OMDb, then Apple; books from Open Library, then Google Books; podcasts from Apple, then gpodder.net; games from RAWG, then OMDb.

If one is blocked or slow where you are, the app quietly tries the next and remembers which one worked on your device. The hint under the title says where the results came from. Games, and the OMDb backups, need free keys in config.js; the setup guide explains how to get them.

How do I log progress?

Use the − and + buttons on a card, or type a number into the box between them. The bar shows how far through you are. When you reach the end, the app asks whether to mark it finished.

Each type counts in its own unit (episodes, chapters, pages, volumes, hours…). You can change the unit in the edit form.

What do the tabs mean?

In progress: you're on it now. Planned: you want to get to it. Paused: on hold. Caught up: you've seen everything that's out and are waiting for more. Finished and Dropped mean what they say. Swipe left or right on a phone to switch tabs.

Games that never end?

For sandbox or online games, tick No ending in the edit form. The card then shows hours played and when you last played, instead of a finish button.

How do rewatches and rereads work?

Open something you've finished and press Start again in the edit form. Your progress resets and the card shows which time through this is. Finishing it again adds to the count (×2, ×3…).

Why is the app asking "Still on these?"

Anything in progress that hasn't been touched for 2 months shows up there. You can keep it, pause it or drop it in one tap, or hide the reminder for 2 weeks.

Together

What's the difference between "Same progress" and "Each at our own pace"?

When you both are in something, it's Together. With Same progress there's one shared progress bar, for things you watch side by side. With Each at our own pace you each have your own progress, status, dates and rating.

Recommendations

In the edit form, tick Recommend to… and add a note if you like. It appears in the other person's Recommendations page, where they can tap Add to my Planned or No thanks. Tick Just recommending if it shouldn't go on your own list.

Up next together

Tick Up next together in the edit form of a Together item to put it in your shared queue at the top of the library. It leaves the queue when you finish it.

Are episode notes spoiler-safe?

Yes. A note is attached to the episode or chapter you were on. The other person only sees it once their own progress has reached that point. Until then it stays hidden.

Genres and tags

What's in Notifications (the bell)?

Everything the other person did that involves you, newest first: comments on episodes, ratings, reviews, recommendations, things they finished, and achievements and levels for both of you. The red number counts what's new since you last opened it, on any device. Tap an entry to open that item. Episode notes from further ahead stay hidden until you get there.

Can I zoom or move a picture?

Yes. When you pick a cover or a profile photo, a frame opens: drag to move the picture, pinch (or use the slider) to zoom, then press Use this. To change a cover later, open the item and tap Adjust (zoom / move) under it.

Why can't I add my own genre?

Genres come from a fixed list of 20, because they count towards your genre badges in the Trophy Room. Tap the ones that fit. For anything else, like "cosmic horror" or "comfort watch", use Tags: you can type whatever you like there. Online search sorts this out for you: "detective" becomes Mystery, and genres that aren't on the list are added as tags.

Anything else goes in Tags. Tags are your own words, like "comfort watch" or "scary at night", and you can filter by them too.

Which genres are there?

Horror, Mystery, Thriller, Psychological, Sci-fi, Fantasy, Action, Adventure, Comedy, Drama, Romance, Slice of life, Crime, Supernatural, Historical, Survival, Mecha, Sports, Music, Non-fiction.

Trophy Room

How do I earn XP?

You earn XP for everything you log, counted straight from your list. The full table is under How XP works in the Trophy Room. In short: time spent (about 10 XP an hour), finishing things, ratings, reviews, episode notes, achievements and genre badges.

What's Our Swamp?

Your shared level, with 15 stages. It grows from things you share: time spent on Together items, finishing them, recommendations one of you took, episode notes, and Together achievements either of you unlocked.

How do genre badges work?

Every genre has a bronze, silver and gold badge, for 5, 15 and 30 finished. Something with two genres counts towards both.

How do achievements work?

Each badge says what it takes, and locked ones show how close you are ("64 / 100"). When you unlock one, a message pops up; tap Trophy Room to jump straight to it.

Badges, XP and levels follow your list. If you delete something, or undo a finish, anything that depended on it goes away again, and comes back (with its message) if you earn it again. A few are about moments, like logging progress at 3 am or finishing something you'd dropped. Those can't be worked out from the list afterwards, so they stay once earned.

Year in review

How are hours estimated?

Roughly: an anime episode is 23 minutes, a TV episode 45, a podcast episode 40, a manga chapter 5, a book page 1.5 minutes, and a movie about 2 hours. Games use the hours you log.

What do the activity calendar and streaks count?

A day counts if you logged progress or finished something that day. Your streak is how many days in a row you did that.

Wrapped

Press ✨ wrapped in Year in review for a summary of your year: what you finished, your top genres, your hours and your favourites. You can share it as text.

💞 Our year, next to it, makes a picture of your year together: what you finished together, your taste match and genre, your best match and biggest disagreement, each of your totals and your Swamp level. Save it or send it straight from there. A reminder shows up in Notifications on 20 December.

Your data

Who can see our list?

Only the two of you. The database only lets in the two email addresses set up for this site.

How do backups work?

Use Backup / restore in your account menu to download a backup file (or send it somewhere on a phone). The app reminds you about once a week. To restore, open the same menu and press Restore from file….

Can I use it like an app on my phone?

Yes. Choose Install as an app in the account menu for step-by-step instructions. The home-screen icon matches your accent colour.

Does it work offline?

Mostly. Your list is saved on the device, so you can look at it and log progress without internet. Changes sync when you're back online. Searching online needs internet.
