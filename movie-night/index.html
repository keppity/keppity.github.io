How to share the film

  1. Whoever has the film opens this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (Mac, Windows or Linux) and loads it.
  2. In the call, share your screen and pick this tab (in Chrome: “Chrome Tab”).
  3. Turn on “Also share tab audio”, otherwise the other person gets picture without sound.
  4. Press Share mode (or T): everything except the film and subtitles disappears, so that's all they see.
